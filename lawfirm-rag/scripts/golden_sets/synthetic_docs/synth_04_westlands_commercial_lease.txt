LEASE OF COMMERCIAL PREMISES

THIS LEASE is made on 15 January 2026

BETWEEN

(1) HIGHRIDGE PROPERTIES LIMITED, a company incorporated in Kenya under registration number PVT-7HQ2K9 and having its registered office at Mvuli Road, Westlands, Nairobi (the "Landlord"); and

(2) MVULE PHARMACY LIMITED, a company incorporated in Kenya under registration number PVT-3RX81LM and having its registered office at Ground Floor, Kipande House Annex, Nairobi (the "Tenant").

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Lease, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Building" means the five-storey commercial building known as Highridge Court erected on Land Reference Number 1870/IX/412, Westlands, Nairobi.

"Premises" means Shop Unit G4 on the ground floor of the Building, measuring approximately 1,850 square feet, as delineated in red on the plan annexed as Schedule 1.

"Commencement Date" means 1 February 2026.

"Term" means six (6) years from the Commencement Date, expiring on 31 January 2032.

"Base Rent" means the rent payable under clause 3.1 as escalated under clause 3.3.

"Service Charge" means the charge payable under clause 4.

"Permitted Use" means a retail pharmacy, including the dispensing of prescription medicines, the sale of over-the-counter medicines, cosmetics and health products, and a consultation room not exceeding 120 square feet.

1.2 Clause headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. GRANT

2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for the Term, together with the right to use the common areas of the Building in common with the Landlord and other tenants, and the right to two (2) designated parking bays numbered P-17 and P-18 in the basement.

3. RENT

3.1 The Tenant shall pay a Base Rent of KES 280,000 per month, exclusive of Value Added Tax, for the first two years of the Term.

3.2 Rent is payable quarterly in advance on or before the fifth (5th) day of each quarter, the quarters commencing on 1 February, 1 May, 1 August and 1 November.

3.3 The Base Rent shall be escalated by seven and a half per cent (7.5%) on the second anniversary of the Commencement Date and every two years thereafter, compounded on the Base Rent then payable. Accordingly the monthly Base Rent shall be KES 301,000 from 1 February 2028 and KES 323,575 from 1 February 2030.

3.4 Rent-free period: the Tenant shall have a rent-free period of two (2) months from the Commencement Date for fitting out the Premises, during which Service Charge shall nevertheless be payable.

3.5 Late payment: any sum not paid within fourteen (14) days of its due date shall attract interest at the rate of two per cent (2%) per month on the outstanding amount until payment.

4. SERVICE CHARGE

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a monthly Service Charge of KES 45,000, exclusive of Value Added Tax, towards the cost of security, cleaning of common areas, lift maintenance, landscaping, common-area electricity and the Building's insurance.

4.2 The Landlord may review the Service Charge annually on 1 February by reference to the actual costs incurred in the preceding year, provided that any increase shall not exceed ten per cent (10%) in any single year.

4.3 The Landlord shall provide the Tenant with an annual statement of Service Charge expenditure, certified by an independent accountant, within four (4) months of the end of each year.

5. DEPOSIT

5.1 On signing this Lease the Tenant shall pay a security deposit equivalent to three (3) months' Base Rent, namely KES 840,000.

5.2 The deposit shall not earn interest and shall be refunded within sixty (60) days after the end of the Term, less any sums properly due to the Landlord for unpaid rent, Service Charge or dilapidations.

6. TENANT'S OBLIGATIONS

6.1 The Tenant shall:
(a) use the Premises only for the Permitted Use;
(b) obtain and maintain at its own cost all licences required for the Permitted Use, including registration of the premises with the Pharmacy and Poisons Board, and shall produce copies of such licences to the Landlord on request;
(c) keep the interior of the Premises, including shop fronts, glazing, internal walls, floors and fixtures, in good and tenantable repair;
(d) not make any structural alterations without the Landlord's prior written consent, and not make any non-structural alterations without giving the Landlord fourteen (14) days' prior written notice;
(e) keep controlled medicines in a locked cabinet in compliance with applicable law;
(f) maintain public liability insurance of not less than KES 20,000,000 per occurrence, naming the Landlord as an additional insured;
(g) operate the pharmacy for not less than ten (10) hours on each weekday and six (6) hours on Saturdays, unless prevented by circumstances beyond its control.

7. LANDLORD'S OBLIGATIONS

7.1 The Landlord shall:
(a) keep the structure, roof, foundations, external walls and common areas of the Building in good repair;
(b) insure the Building against fire, flood, riot and other usual risks for its full reinstatement value;
(c) provide a standby generator capable of supplying the Premises' lighting and refrigeration circuits within thirty (30) seconds of a power outage;
(d) permit the Tenant quiet enjoyment of the Premises without interruption.

7A. UTILITIES

7A.1 Electricity to the Premises is supplied through a sub-meter installed by the Landlord. The Tenant shall pay for electricity consumed at the prevailing tariff charged to the Landlord by the supplier, plus an administration fee of five per cent (5%), within fourteen (14) days of the Landlord's monthly invoice.

7A.2 Water is supplied to the Premises through a separate sub-meter and charged at cost without any administration fee. The Landlord shall maintain a reserve water tank of not less than 20,000 litres serving the ground floor.

7A.3 The Tenant may install its own internet and telephone lines through the Building's riser ducts at its own cost, using a contractor approved by the Landlord.

7B. SIGNAGE

7B.1 The Tenant may display one illuminated fascia sign above the shop front not exceeding 2.4 metres in width and 0.6 metres in height, and one projecting sign displaying the green cross pharmacy symbol, subject to County Government of Nairobi advertising approval obtained by the Tenant at its own cost.

7B.2 No signs, posters, or promotional material shall be affixed to the exterior glazing other than opening hours and statutory notices, save that promotional displays occupying not more than twenty per cent (20%) of the glazing area may be placed inside the shop window.

7C. REINSTATEMENT AND DILAPIDATIONS

7C.1 At the end of the Term, however determined, the Tenant shall remove its fixtures, shelving, signage, and the consultation room partitions, and shall reinstate the Premises to the condition shown in the schedule of condition signed by the parties on the Commencement Date, fair wear and tear excepted.

7C.2 The Tenant shall not be required to remove the refrigeration and air-conditioning units installed during fit-out if the Landlord elects, by written notice given not later than three (3) months before the end of the Term, to retain them without compensation.

7C.3 If the Tenant fails to reinstate, the Landlord may carry out the works and recover the reasonable cost from the deposit and, to the extent the deposit is insufficient, from the Tenant as a debt.

8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or part with possession of the whole or any part of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld in the case of an assignment to a company of comparable financial standing that will continue the Permitted Use.

8.2 Any change in the controlling shareholding of the Tenant shall be treated as an assignment for the purposes of this clause.

9. EXCLUSIVITY

9.1 For so long as the Tenant is not in breach of this Lease, the Landlord shall not let any other unit in the Building for use as a pharmacy or chemist.

10. TERMINATION

10.1 Break clause: the Tenant may terminate this Lease with effect from the third anniversary of the Commencement Date, namely 1 February 2029, by giving the Landlord not less than six (6) months' prior written notice, provided that all rent and Service Charge have been paid up to the break date. A break fee equal to two (2) months' Base Rent shall be payable.

10.2 Forfeiture: the Landlord may re-enter and terminate this Lease if any rent remains unpaid for thirty (30) days after its due date and the Tenant fails to pay within a further fifteen (15) days after written demand, or if the Tenant commits any other material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice specifying the breach.

10.3 Insolvency: the Landlord may terminate if the Tenant is placed in liquidation or administration, other than for a solvent reorganisation.

10.4 Destruction: if the Premises are destroyed or made unfit for use by an insured risk and are not reinstated within nine (9) months, either party may terminate on one (1) month's written notice, and rent shall cease from the date of destruction.

11. RENEWAL

11.1 The Tenant shall have an option to renew this Lease for a further term of five (5) years on the same terms, save as to rent, which shall be the open market rent agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, determined by a valuer nominated by the Chairman of the Institution of Surveyors of Kenya. The option is exercisable by written notice given not earlier than twelve (12) months and not later than nine (9) months before the end of the Term.

11A. LANDLORD'S RIGHT OF ENTRY

11A.1 The Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises on giving the Tenant not less than forty-eight (48) hours' written notice, accompanied by a representative of the Tenant and outside the Tenant's peak trading hours of 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., to inspect the condition of the Premises, to carry out repairs that are the Landlord's responsibility, or, in the last six (6) months of the Term, to show the Premises to prospective tenants.

11A.2 In an emergency threatening life or property, the Landlord may enter at any time without notice, but shall inform the Tenant as soon as reasonably practicable and shall not, in any event, open the controlled medicines store.

11B. FORCE MAJEURE

11B.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations, other than obligations to pay money, to the extent the failure is caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including riot, civil commotion, epidemic, government lockdown orders, or failure of the national electricity grid for more than seventy-two (72) consecutive hours.

11B.2 If a government order prohibits the Tenant from opening the Premises to the public for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, the Base Rent (but not the Service Charge) shall be reduced by fifty per cent (50%) for the period of the prohibition in excess of those thirty days. A pharmacy permitted to operate as an essential service during such an order shall not be treated as prohibited from opening.

11C. VALUE ADDED TAX AND WITHHOLDING TAX

11C.1 All sums payable under this Lease are exclusive of Value Added Tax, which the Tenant shall pay at the prevailing rate, currently sixteen per cent (16%), against a valid tax invoice.

11C.2 Where the Tenant is required by law to withhold tax on rent, it shall do so and deliver the withholding certificate to the Landlord within thirty (30) days; the amount withheld shall be treated as paid to the Landlord.

11D. REGISTRATION

11D.1 This Lease, being for a term exceeding two (2) years, shall be registered against the title to the Building. The Landlord shall procure the registration within ninety (90) days of execution, and the Tenant shall bear the stamp duty and registration fees.

12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

12.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute by negotiation between their senior representatives within twenty-one (21) days of a written notice of dispute.

12.2 Any dispute not so resolved shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator in Nairobi under the Arbitration Act, 1995. The arbitrator shall be appointed by agreement or, failing agreement within fourteen (14) days, by the Chairman of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch). The language of the arbitration shall be English.

13. GOVERNING LAW

13.1 This Lease is governed by the laws of Kenya.

14. STAMP DUTY AND COSTS

14.1 Stamp duty on this Lease shall be borne by the Tenant. Each party shall bear its own legal costs of negotiating and completing this Lease.

15. NOTICES

15.1 Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, registered post, or email to the addresses set out above, or, for email, to legal@highridge.example for the Landlord and directors@mvulepharmacy.example for the Tenant.

SCHEDULE 2 — FIT-OUT RULES

1. Before starting any fit-out works, the Tenant shall submit to the Landlord's building manager detailed drawings, a programme of works, and the names of all contractors, and shall not begin works until the Landlord has approved them in writing. The Landlord shall respond within ten (10) working days of a complete submission.

2. The Tenant shall pay a refundable fit-out deposit of KES 150,000 before works begin, to be held against damage to common areas caused by the Tenant's contractors. The deposit shall be refunded within thirty (30) days after practical completion of the fit-out, less the cost of making good any such damage.

3. Noisy works, including drilling, cutting, and demolition, may only be carried out between 7:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m. on weekdays, or at any time on Sundays, so as not to disturb other occupiers during trading hours.

4. All electrical works shall be carried out by a contractor licensed by the Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority, and a test certificate shall be delivered to the Landlord before the Premises open for trade.

5. Contractors shall use the service lift only and shall protect the lift interior and common-area floors with boarding.

6. The fit-out shall include a hand-wash basin in the consultation room and a separate secure store for controlled medicines with no external window.

SCHEDULE 3 — BUILDING RULES

1. The Building is open to the public from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. every day. Tenants may access their premises outside those hours using an access card issued by the building manager.

2. Deliveries to ground-floor units shall be made through the rear service entrance before 10:00 a.m. or after 4:00 p.m. Delivery vehicles may not wait in the customer parking area.

3. General waste shall be placed in the Building's central refuse room daily. Pharmaceutical and clinical waste, including expired medicines and used sharps, shall not be placed in the central refuse room and shall be collected by a licensed hazardous waste contractor at least once a month, at the Tenant's cost, with collection records kept for inspection.

4. Smoking is prohibited throughout the Building, including the basement parking and the rooftop.

5. Tenants shall not obstruct the corridors, fire exits, or fire-fighting equipment, and shall participate in the fire drill held twice a year.

6. The designated parking bays are for the Tenant's staff and may not be sublet. Customer parking is available on the open lot on a first-come basis and is free for the first ninety (90) minutes.

Total budget: KES 12,300,000 per year. The Tenant's 4.4% share is KES 541,200 per year, which the Landlord has rounded down to KES 540,000, being KES 45,000 per month.
